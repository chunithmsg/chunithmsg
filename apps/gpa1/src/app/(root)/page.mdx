# Rules

## Overview

The **Gauntlet of Pure Accuracy (GPA) 1.0** will be held on **Saturday, 30th August 2025,** 1pm (SG Time), and would be expected to end by 6.30pm.

Qualifiers will be conducted in an **own time, own target** format.
Players are allowed to play the specified set of 3 qualifier tracks at **any Virtualand or FUNTAN outlet** anytime **from 04 - 24 August 2025.**

In this event, only the **Top 18 players** in the Qualifiers will proceed into a set of 4 Swiss Rounds,
where each round has 6 matches of 3-player groups, and all players play one match per round in a **1v1v1 format.**
The **Top 9 players** after the 4 Swiss Rounds will move on to the Grand Finals consisting of 4 matches,
where each match will consist of the **bottom 3 players** on the leaderboard,
with every match **eliminating the 2 lowest scoring players** and the **next 2 players** from the bottom of the leaderboard **replacing them** for the next match.

## Scoring System

<br />
<span style={{ color: 'red', fontSize: '14px' }}>
  *Note: This event uses a scoring system different from the traditional
  Score-Attack format*
</span>

This event will use a scoring system that **heavily favours accuracy.** The maximum possible score per song will be **zero,**
with point deductions for judgements below "Justice Critical" as follows:

- <span style={{ color: '#f28b1b' }}>Justice</span>: -1 Point
- <span style={{ color: '#00bf63' }}>Attack</span>: -5 Points
- <span style={{ color: '#737373' }}>Miss</span>: -3 Points

### Attempting Qualifiers

Qualifiers can only be attempted in **Virtualand or FUNTAN outlets**, only **during the Qualifiers period** (04 - 24 August 2025),
and only **after the registration fee has been paid.**
Unless special permission is granted by the Organisers, **any entry that violates these conditions will be disregarded.**

Please note the following points for a valid qualifier attempt:

- The qualifier songs **must be played in the exact order** as shown above (i.e. **STRICTLY** Tracks 1, 2, and 3. Playing the songs on Tracks 2, 3, and 4 will **NOT** count as a valid qualifier attempt).
- **Pictures of the 3 scores must be taken.** Each picture must show the entire result screen together with **the Virtualand card reader.**
- The 3 pictures must be submitted **together with a CHUNITHM-NET** play history screenshot clearly showing the 3 scores in order.
- All pictures should be submitted together **on the event Discord server in your own submission thread.** (More details in the Discord server)

### Ranking

Rankings will be based on **total point deductions** across all 3 songs.
We will **only take into account players' best submission** for the final Qualifier rankings.

In the possible event of a tie in point deductions, the player with the **earlier submission time** will be ranked higher.

The **Top 18 ranked players** will be invited to the main event on 30th August.

### Other Notes

#### Fair Play

The organisers reserve the right to disqualify any player from the event should they be found to be cheating.

Examples of cheating (not limited to):

- Having another player attempt qualifiers in your place or assist your qualifier run in any way
- Editing submission pictures or screenshots

#### Waiting List

Players **outside of Top 18 in the Qualifiers** will be placed on a waiting list.
In the event that anyone from the Top 18 is unable to make it for the main event,
waiting list players **may be called up to fill in the missing spot, starting from the 19th ranked.**

#### Overseas Registrants

Players who reside overseas may register and play the qualifiers, but **must inform the organisers before attempting.**
Qualified overseas players **must be willing to travel to Singapore to attend the main event at their own expense.**

## FAQs

### Will the games be sponsored?

The game credits will be provided for the **main event only.** Qualifier runs will be at the players' own expenses.

### When will the Waiting List people know whether they will be competing in the Finals?

On the day of the competition, if there are no-shows, the organisers will contact you directly.

We highly recommend coming for the main event (as a spectator) even if your score falls below the Top 18 players.

### What is the Song Pool?

We will cut off the Song Pool at the Kasane Teto update. (17 Jul 2025 Update)

### Is there any particular character skill needed for the Tournament?

We recommend using the **Boost or Combo skill**.
We are not responsible if you accidentally pick a Danger or above Skill and fail out during the Tournament itself.
Should a participant fail a song prematurely due to a Danger Skill,
we will **treat the rest of the notes as Misses (-3)** as the fail out penalty.
